| Component | Employer | Employee | Total |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------|

### CPF Salary Ceiling
- Ordinary Wage ceiling: SGD 6,800/month (raised from SGD 6,300 in Jan 2024)
- Additional Wage ceiling: SGD 102,000 annually minus total OW subject to CPF

## Employment Act — Statutory Minimums

### Annual Leave (Section 43A)
- 7 days after 1 year of service
- +1 day per additional year, capped at 14 days
- Applies to employees earning ≤ SGD 4,500/month (managers/execs covered by contract)

### Sick Leave (Section 89)
- 14 days outpatient sick leave per year
- 60 days hospitalization leave (inclusive of the 14 outpatient days)
- Requires medical certificate from approved doctor

### Maternity Leave (CDLA)
- 16 weeks for Singapore citizen/PR children
- First 8 weeks: employer-funded
- Last 8 weeks: Government-Paid Maternity Leave (GPML), capped at SGD 10,000/week
- Eligibility: 3 months continuous service

### Paternity Leave (GPPL)
- 2 weeks Government-Paid Paternity Leave
- Capped at SGD 2,500/week
- Eligibility: child is Singapore citizen, 3 months continuous service

### Childcare Leave
- 6 days/year per parent (child < 7 years old)
- First 3 days: employer-funded; last 3 days: government-funded
- 2 days extended childcare leave (child 7–12 years)

## MOM Guidelines — Benefits Best Practices

### Tripartite Standards on Employment Practices
- Flexible work arrangements (from Dec 2024: formal request process required)
- Grievance handling procedures
- Recruitment practices (anti-discrimination)

### Medical Benefits
- No statutory requirement for outpatient medical benefits (beyond sick leave)
- Market practice: ~98% of employers provide outpatient coverage
- Common structures: managed care, panel clinic, co-payment models

### Insurance Benefits
- Work Injury Compensation Act (WICA): mandatory for manual workers and employees earning ≤ SGD 2,600/month
- Group Term Life: not statutory but 94% market prevalence
- Group Hospitalization & Surgical: not statutory but 97% market prevalence

## Cost Impact Rules

When calculating statutory cost baseline:
1. CPF is the largest mandatory cost component (~17% of OW for employer)
2. Statutory leave valued at: (monthly salary / 21.75 working days) × days
3. WICA premium: typically SGD 0.25–1.50 per SGD 100 of insured wages (industry-dependent)

## Regulatory Constraints on Optimization
- Cannot reduce CPF below statutory rates (criminal offense)
- Cannot reduce annual leave below statutory minimums
- Flexible benefits must still meet statutory floor for each component
- Changes to employment terms require employee consent (Employment Act Section 8)
